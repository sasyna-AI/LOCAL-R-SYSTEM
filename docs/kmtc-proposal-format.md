# KMTC Research Proposal Format

This format is based on the proposal supplied by the user. It is the required
proposal structure for SASYNA; do not reorder or silently omit sections.

## Preliminary pages

1. Cover page
2. Certification
3. Declaration
4. Dedication
5. Acknowledgment
6. Table of Contents
7. List of Abbreviations and Acronyms
8. Definition of Terms
9. Abstract

## Chapter One: Introduction

- 1.1 Introduction
- 1.2 Background Information
- 1.3 Problem Statement
- 1.4 Justification
- 1.5 Research Questions
- 1.6 Broad Objectives
- 1.6.1 Specific Objectives
- 1.7 Scope of the Study
- 1.8 Significance of the Study
- 1.9 Study Limitations
- 1.10 Study Delimitation
- 1.11 Assumptions
- 1.12 Conceptual Framework

## Chapter Two: Literature Review

The sample uses topic-specific, numbered review subsections. Keep the chapter
heading and allow the subsection wording to reflect the student's research
topic and objectives.

## Chapter Three: Research Methodology

- 3.0 Overview
- 3.1 Study Design
- 3.2 Study Area
- 3.3 Study Population
- 3.4 Sampling Technique
- 3.5 Inclusion Criteria
- 3.6 Exclusion Criteria
- 3.7 Variables
  - 3.7.1 Dependent Variable
  - 3.7.2 Independent Variables
- 3.8 Sample Size Determination
- 3.9 Data Collection Process
- 3.10 Data Collection Process (duplicate in supplied sample; flag for review)
- 3.11 Piloting
- 3.12 Validity
- 3.13 Reliability
- 3.14 Data Analysis
- 3.15 Ethical Considerations

## End matter

1. References
2. Appendices
   - Appendix I: Research Checklist
   - Appendix II: Budget
   - Appendix III: Work Plan
   - Appendix IV: Map of the Study Area

## Formatting notes

- Paper: US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches)
- Margins: 1 inch on all sides
- Body style: Times New Roman, 12 pt
- Preserve the sample's front-matter/chapter order, heading hierarchy, and
  appendix sequence.
- The supplied document contains a duplicate methodology heading at 3.9/3.10.
  Surface this as a template warning for the student/admin rather than
  silently correcting or expanding it.
- A dissertation must use its own approved template; do not infer it from this
  proposal format.
